About ParlaySplit

ParlaySplit is a free social coordination tool built for sports bettors who share parlays with friends, group chats, Discord servers, and online betting communities.

The idea came from a simple problem: people constantly tail the exact same sportsbook slips, but managing participation manually becomes messy fast.

Tracking who is in, how much everyone contributed, estimated payout shares, and which parlay everyone is following usually happens through scattered screenshots, Venmo messages, Discord chats, Reddit threads, and Twitter replies. ParlaySplit was built to simplify that process.

What You Can Do

  • Create shareable parlay pages
  • Upload sportsbook screenshots
  • Paste betting slip details
  • Track group participation
  • Estimate payout shares automatically
  • Organize parlays in one place

The platform is designed for bettors using sportsbooks like FanDuel, DraftKings, BetMGM, Caesars Sportsbook, Kalshi, Polymarket, ESPN Bet, and basically any sportsbook.

Why ParlaySplit Exists

Sports betting has become increasingly social.

People share:

  • Same game parlays
  • Player props
  • Moneyline picks
  • MLB parlays
  • NBA parlays
  • NFL bets

Across Reddit, Discord, Twitter/X, and group chats every day.

But the tools around sportsbook sharing and group coordination are still fragmented. ParlaySplit focuses on making sportsbook slip sharing easier, cleaner, and more organized.

Built For Simplicity

The goal of ParlaySplit is not to replace sportsbooks or betting apps.

ParlaySplit does not accept wagers, process payments, or operate as a sportsbook. Instead, it acts as a lightweight utility layer for organizing and sharing parlays socially.

The platform is intentionally simple:

  • No subscription paywalls
  • No VIP pick packages
  • No gambling advice
  • No cluttered dashboards

Just a straightforward way to share and coordinate parlays online.

The Future of ParlaySplit

The platform is still early and evolving quickly based on community feedback.

Future features may include:

  • Sportsbook slip importing
  • Discord integrations
  • Mobile notifications
  • Improved screenshot parsing
  • Trending public parlays
  • Live participation tracking

ParlaySplit is currently focused on improving the experience of sharing parlays with friends and online betting communities.
